windows support: compiles and runs on real windows hardware now (two
machines confirmed), not just CI. fixed along the way: no opengl
fallback backend (added wgpu's "webgl" feature, which despite the name
also covers native windows opengl via wgl), a console window flashing
on every launch (windows_subsystem = "windows"), curl_chrome136 not
being found unless it's on PATH (now also checked next to the exe
itself). still only tested on a couple of machines, not broad
hardware/driver coverage.

yuma (aniwatchtv.to) stream resolution: search() and episodes() work,
watch() is not implemented and there are no plans to add it.

anilist tracker: two-way now. whoami/connect/sync-toggle push local
progress up, and my_list() pulls your CURRENT/REPEATING list back down,
shown as its own row on the discover tab (click one to jump to
search). no in-app editing of anilist entries beyond what playing an
episode already pushes.
